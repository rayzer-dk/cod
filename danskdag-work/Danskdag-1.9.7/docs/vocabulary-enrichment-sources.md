# Vocabulary enrichment review

All 838 verified entries from the supplied workbook have authored Danish contexts, Russian context translations and semantic associations. The original 55 contexts are retained; four supplementary modules provide the other 783. Every imported ID and original source text survives integration. Unverified entries and ambiguous notes remain excluded from exercises.

The supplementary content was reviewed entry by entry on 2026-10-23. Den Danske Ordbog was consulted for uncertain meanings and usage, including skid, pårørende, klæder, oplysning, udsigt and løsne:

Reviewed practice corrections use explicit practiceDa/practiceRu fields; the learner can still trace the original import. New contexts and associations were authored for the app rather than copied dictionary quotations. Inflected contextual targets have explicit answers where removing the base lemma would be incorrect. All 838 verified entries generate a reconstructable contextual gap without leaving another answer occurrence visible.

Thirty-six illustrated words have original local SVG drawings and authored example sentences. The fork entry teaches en gravegreb, including the same article in its example. Technical drawings are memory cues rather than equipment identification or operating instructions.

Review and automated completeness checks reduce errors but are not independent native-speaker certification. Association imagery is an editorial cue, not an etymological claim or a validated optimal mnemonic for every learner.
