# Danskdag reference expansion

Extend the existing reference hub so the learner can browse and hear common everyday words by category and understand how tense forms are used. Preserve existing lessons, storage keys, progress IDs, theme and Site audience.

Common words are a curated practical selection, not a ranked corpus frequency list or a claim of complete vocabulary coverage. Reuse the verb and adjective tables. New noun entries have four explicit gender/number forms; other entries provide context and usage notes. Search and category filtering happen before pagination. Each Danish headword, form and example has independent audio.

Training and saved marks refer to complete contextual examples, avoiding ambiguity when the same headword has different word classes or senses. New example IDs remain stable. Existing duplicate sentences share dictionary aliases through the existing engine.

Tense cards reuse the five existing tense lessons and their examples. Infinitive and imperative remain verb forms, not tenses. Each card opens its actual lesson. Future reference retains present tense, skal and vil distinctions.

Provide direct shortcuts from Grammar and My Words, and a six-tab reference hub. Maintain usable 360px/20px layouts, both themes and contained horizontal tables. Release as 1.5.1 with the complete application ZIP.
