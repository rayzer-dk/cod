# Pronunciation practice, 1.7.0

Ten original guides use Danish words, original sentences, Russian explanations and manual listening checkpoints. The chunks concatenate to the written sentence; they are learning segments, not a phonetic or prosodic annotation. Meanings are contextual teaching translations. No IPA or Russian sound transcription is generated.

Primary checks, accessed 2026-10-25:

- Sproget.dk, [Bogstaver og lyde](https://sproget.dk/sprogviden/sprogtemaer/udtale/bogstaver-og-lyde/): vowel articulation, spelling versus sound, and the sound in mad. Used for y/u, the qualified vowel comparisons and soft d.
- Sproget.dk, [Tryk, længde og stød](https://sproget.dk/sprogviden/sprogtemaer/udtale/tryk-laengde-og-stoed/): stress contrasts, vowel length, stød, and dialect variation. Used for billigst/bilist and for the stød contrasts man/mand and hun/hund (without stød / with stød). The earlier verb/noun køber pair was removed in 1.9.1: it does not illustrate stød and the page was not shown to confirm it. Exact stød notation for hun/hund should still be checked against ordnet.dk (see `docs/release-1.9.1-content.md`).
- Sproget.dk, [Assimilation](https://sproget.dk/sprogviden/sprogtemaer/udtale/assimilation-naar-lyde-smelter-sammen/): weak e and neighbouring sounds. The guide avoids an instruction to omit every final e.
- DSL, Den Danske Ordbog: [lys](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/lys), [lus](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/lus), [læse and læsse](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/l%C3%A6s), [bo](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/bo), [sø](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/s%C3%B8), [leve](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/leve). Used to check specific contrasts and meanings. The ø/o and æ/e selections are not labelled minimal pairs.
- John Tøndering, [Skitser af prosodi i spontant dansk](https://www.danpass.hum.ku.dk/links_2014/publications/johnt_thesis.pdf), University of Copenhagen, 2008: Danish spontaneous speech has prosodic phrasing and variation. The rhythm activity therefore asks the learner to hear the actual utterance rather than imposing a universal stress pattern.
- Nikolai Pharao, [Consonant Reduction in Copenhagen Danish](https://www.danpass.hum.ku.dk/links_2014/publications/pharao_thesis.pdf), University of Copenhagen: reductions in spontaneous speech vary with phonetic environment, stress, lexical frequency and speaker. The reduction activity is recognition practice, not a rule to delete a fixed set of sounds. It keeps ordinary spelling in TTS fields and does not promise the synthesizer will produce colloquial reductions. Existing speech-imitating spelling checks are documented separately in `docs/spoken-danish-sources.md`.

Method: listen to a word contrast, repeat a short phrase in chunks, say the whole phrase, record, listen back, and compare one named feature. This is a teaching design, not a validated pronunciation assessment. The existing system voice can differ by device and may not render all native details reliably. Stød practice directs the learner to a native dictionary recording when its contrast is unclear. The guide supplies no automatic score, accent judgement, or claim that TTS is native high-variability phonetic training.

Recordings are handled by the parent recorder implementation, separately from this content. This file does not enable capture, persist recordings, call a provider or award recall credit.
