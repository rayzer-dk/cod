# Quantity, verb constructions and informal comprehension

Primary reference: Den Danske Ordbog (DDO), Det Danske Sprog- og Litteraturselskab. Checks performed on 2026-10-24, with particular attention to separate constructions versus written compounds, argument patterns and register. All Danish sentences, Russian translations, rules and distractors are original teaching material. Dictionary examples have not been copied. CEFR placement and the three intensity bands are editorial teaching labels, not DDO ratings.

The data module contains 10 lessons with 6 exercises each, 20 reference families with 84 construction entries, and 12 optional informal-expression entries. Lesson levels: A1 × 1, A2 × 4, B1 × 4, B2 × 1.

## Quantity and comparison

| Teaching distinction | Primary entries and verified point |
| --- | --- |
| lille / små | [lille](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/lille): singular lille, plural små, comparative mindre. This concerns size; it is not the count/mass quantity distinction. |
| meget / mange | [meget](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/meget), [mange](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/mange): amount/degree versus a large number. DDO resolves meget through megen and mange through mangen; everyday meget is not restricted to neuter nouns. |
| mere / flere | [meget](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/meget), [mange](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/mange): more amount/degree versus more individual units. The exercises use unambiguous kaffe and stole contexts. |
| lidt / få | [lidt](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/lidt), [få](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/f%C3%A5): a small amount/degree versus a small number. Få meaning “few” is the plural adjective, not the homonymous verb “receive”. |
| mindre / færre | [mindre](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/mindre), [få](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/f%C3%A5): mindre is also the comparative of lille; få has færre/færrest. The lesson teaches mindre strøm / færre biler without asserting that every real-life count use of mindre is impossible. |

## Whole constructions, particles and compounds

A family is a comparison aid, not an etymological claim that every displayed form can be generated mechanically from the heading. The module includes particles, prepositional complements, fixed expressions and inseparable written verbs. These are identified in the notes rather than all being labelled “separable prefixes”. For example, på in se på introduces an object; sig in melde sig ind is required by that construction. A German separable-prefix rule must not be transferred to Danish.

The prefix entry [for-](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/for-) itself distinguishes different headwords and several senses. The teaching rule therefore does not assign a fixed Russian translation to every occurrence of for-, an-, op-, ud- or another initial element.

| Contrast checked in detail | Primary sources and editorial decision |
| --- | --- |
| give / give op / give ud / afgive / udgive | [give](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/give), [give op](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/59003139), [afgive](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/afgive), [udgive](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/udgive). Spending uses give BELØB ud; publishing uses the written compound udgive. Afgive sin stemme is voting, not surrendering. |
| finde / finde ud af / finde på | [finde](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/finde). Finde ud af takes a noun or an embedded question/clause; the “work out how” use also occurs with at + infinitive. Keep ud af together as part of the learned construction. |
| holde af / holde på / holde op med / holde ud | [holde](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/holde). Affection, retaining heat, stopping an activity and enduring something are distinct constructions. Holde på also has other readings, including insisting on a view. |
| lægge på / lægge ned / nedlægge | [lægge](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/l%C3%A6gge), [nedlægge](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/nedl%C3%A6gge). Lægge på can end a call; physically laying a cabinet down is distinct from closing a department. |
| tage af / tage på / prøve på | [tage](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/tage), [prøve](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/pr%C3%B8ve). Clothing objects stand before af/på in the examples. Tage på also concerns weight; prøve NOGET (på) supplies the try-on construction. |
| bygge om / ombygge | [bygge](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/bygge). DDO lists ombygge as a synonym of bygge om. Similar meaning does not imply identical word order in every argument pattern. |
| skrive under / underskrive | [skrive](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/skrive). DDO gives both skrive (NOGET) under and skrive under på NOGET; the compound underskrive takes the document directly. |
| melde sig til / tilmelde sig / melde sig ind | [melde](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/melde), [tilmelde](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/tilmelde). Tilmelding may use a direct course/event complement or til NOGET; neither is marked universally incorrect. Club membership is taught as melde sig ind i. |
| komme an på / ankomme | [komme](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/komme), [ankomme](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/ankomme). Dependency and arrival are different meanings and written forms. An- remains inside ankommer/ankom. |
| anmelde / ansætte / forandre | [anmelde](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/anmelde), [ansætte](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/ans%C3%A6tte), [forandre](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/forandre). Teach complete verbs; anmelde can mean formally report or review, depending on its object. |

Additional base entries consulted for the family collection: [gå](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/g%C3%A5), [stå](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/st%C3%A5), [sætte](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/s%C3%A6tte), [levere](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/levere), [stille](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/stille), [tale](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/tale), [køre](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/k%C3%B8re), [se](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/se), [slå](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/sl%C3%A5). Literal directions are presented alongside idiomatic readings; ind/inde and ned/nede distinguish direction from location.

## Optional informal comprehension

These entries are exported separately from patternLessons. They are a manually opened comprehension reference, with contextual meaning, register, a neutral alternative and an original example. They are not normal daily-course exercises or automatic new-word suggestions. The integration layer must preserve that boundary. Insult examples do not target another person or a protected group.

Intensity depends on intonation, relationship, audience and situation. Mild/moderate/strong are approximate app bands; especially sgu and expressive first elements vary substantially between speakers. Their Russian labels describe the example and do not assert a universal word-for-word equivalence.

| Entry | Primary source and relevant distinction |
| --- | --- |
| for pokker | [pokker](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/pokker): a less strong oath; anger, annoyance or another feeling. |
| for søren | [søren](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/s%C3%B8ren): explicitly a mild oath, often for annoyance or surprise. |
| sgu | [sgu](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/sgu): an intensifying oath marking a personal attitude or assurance, especially in speech. |
| fandens | [fandens](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/fandens): irritation and intensifying uses; not always a literal adjective meaning “bad”. |
| for helvede | [helvede](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/helvede): an emotional oath; the app marks the displayed expression strong. |
| hold kæft | [kæft](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/k%C3%A6ft): distinguish the rude silence command from an expressive exclamation of surprise, disapproval or another strong feeling. |
| pisse- | [pisse-](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/pisse-): informal intensifying first element. The lexical notation has a hyphen; the example compound pissekoldt is written together. |
| skide- | [skide-](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/skide-): an informal intensifier, including positive adjectives. Skidegod is written together. |
| lort | [lort](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/lort): literal substance, an object/situation judged bad, and a derogatory person label are different uses. The app example concerns a poor-quality cable. |
| fuck | [fuck](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/fuck): a very informal exclamation; the example is a reaction to an accident, with no addressee. |
| idiot | [idiot](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/idiot): often an insult. The example is self-directed; the neutral alternative describes a mistake. |
| shit | [shit](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/shit): the interjection conveys surprise, annoyance or related feelings, distinct from its noun uses. |

## Validation

`node --test tests/patterns-data.test.mjs` checks lesson/item identity uniqueness, completeness, exact gap reconstruction, one accepted displayed option, accepted Danish/Russian full answers, 20 complete reference families, separation of optional expressions, and answerability through createTasks in easy/mixed/expert/auto modes. Every lesson has six contextual gaps with four distinct options; all 60 complete Danish exercise sentences are unique after normalization.
