# Common vocabulary and tense reference, 1.5.1

The list is an editorial selection for daily situations, not a numerical frequency ranking. It does not claim that all popular words are included. The original bilingual examples and explanations are written for this application. Existing 114 verb paradigms and 40 adjective/quantity paradigms are reused rather than copied into another word list.

The new selection contains 101 entries: 25 nouns, 20 adverbs, 23 pronouns, 12 prepositions, 12 conjunctions and nine question words/combinations. Question words are a functional learning category, not a single formal word class. Identical headwords across distinct word classes (for as a preposition/conjunction) retain separate contextual examples. Saved marks and practice use the complete example sentence, with ordinary catalog deduplication for identical sentences.

Noun forms are explicit, never guessed from the headword: indefinite singular including en/et, definite singular, indefinite plural, definite plural. Examples include barn/barnet/børn/børnene, cykel/cyklen/cykler/cyklerne, and tog/toget/tog/togene. For job, the displayed plural jobs is one accepted variant; job is also permitted. This is a finite learning paradigm, not an exhaustive dictionary entry.

Primary sources checked on 24 October 2026:

- Dansk Sprognævn, [Grammatiske ord](https://sproget.dk/typiske-problemer/grammatiske-ord/): word classes and tense names. The five tense cards reuse the existing reviewed formLessons rules/examples. Future reference includes present with a future expression and modal constructions; infinitive and imperative are not labelled as tenses.
- Dansk Sprognævn, [Hans, hendes eller sin](https://sproget.dk/typiske-problemer/hans-hendes-eller-sin/): the basic standard model for sin refers to a third-person singular subject in its own clause. The app does not prescribe it for every plural or exceptional construction.
- DDO, [job](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/job), [barn](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/barn), [cykel](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/cykel), [nøgle](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/nøgle), [ting](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/ting): selected inflection checks and sense distinctions.
- DDO, [så](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/så): conjunction så introduces a consequent main clause (så jeg går), while adverbial så in the first position uses inversion (så går jeg). The distinction is explicit in the note.
