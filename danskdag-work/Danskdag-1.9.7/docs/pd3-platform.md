# PD3 platform notes

Code: `dist/pd3-page.mjs` (controller), `pd3-views.mjs` and `pd3-practice-views.mjs` (HTML), `pd3-reader-view.mjs` (reader markup and DOM updates),
`pd3-audio.mjs` (sentence-by-sentence reader), `pd3-diff.mjs` (dictation comparison), `pd3-writing-check.mjs`, `pd3-store.mjs`, `pd3-data.mjs` (content aggregator), `pd3.css`.

Storage: since 1.9.3 PD3 results are the optional field `state.pd3` of the learner's progress (`danskdag.progress.v1`): they are part of the JSON export/import, recovery copies, cloud sync, tab merging (`pd3-merge.mjs`), the "Прогресс" page, XP, streak and the day goal (`awardPd3` in `engine.mjs`). The schema and its size limits (about 256 KB, drafts shortened, at most 300 records per map) live in `engine.mjs`, so the Worker validates the same data; the field is absent until PD3 is first used, so existing progress files and hashes are unchanged.
The old key `danskdag.pd3.v1` (1.9.2) is migrated once at start (`pd3-migrate.mjs`): merged into `state.pd3`, copied to `danskdag.pd3.v1.migrated`, removed only after the progress was saved. "Скачать копию PD3 / Загрузить" in the hub work with `state.pd3`; the file format is unchanged (new optional fields: `plan`, `stats`, `xp`).
XP: first completion and each improvement of the best result pay a share of the activity's worth (`PD3_XP`), nothing below 30 %, at most 60 XP a day from PD3; `state.pd3.xp` remembers what was paid. Hub additions: exam plan (`plan.examDate`, `pd3-progress.mjs` rule), frequent dictation mistakes (`stats.dictation`), "add to my words" in the reader (`pd3-vocab.mjs`).
Voice recordings are never stored: they live in memory (blob URL) and are cleared on leaving the page, hiding the tab or closing it.

Reader: one `SpeechSynthesisUtterance` per sentence. The current word comes from `boundary` events; when none arrives within 700 ms the reader estimates word times
from character counts and the rate, and calibrates the estimate with the measured duration of finished sentences. Pause is cancel plus restart of the current sentence.
Only one sound at a time: the reader claims the output through `claimSpeech()` in `speech.mjs`, and `speak()`/`stopSpeech()` stop it.

Recorded audio (optional): a text may carry `audio:{src,marks:[{s,e},...]}` (seconds per sentence, same length as `sentences`). The page then plays the file and highlights by `timeupdate`.
Put the files in `dist/` (for example `dist/audio/<id>.mp3`); `tools/asset-types.mjs` gives `audio/mpeg`, `audio/ogg`, `audio/wav` and stores them as binary in the hosted worker.
Limits: the standalone `Danskdag.html` does not embed audio files (`media-src blob:`), so there the reader always uses the device voice; the hosted service worker does not
precache audio and the hosted worker does not answer HTTP range requests. A file that fails to load falls back to the device voice.

## QA follow-up notes

- Dictation comparison: invisible characters (ZWSP, soft hyphen, LRM/RLM, bidi controls) are removed before tokenising; the score is capped at 99% while anything is wrong; an input too large for the alignment table returns `tooLong` (the page asks to shorten it) instead of throwing. The field length is 1.5 times the reference.
- Unfinished task: `danskdag.pd3.run.v1` (device-only, not in the shared progress, the PD3 copy or the cloud) keeps a dictation text, used listens, reading/listening answers and an exam (answers, task, absolute deadline) for 24 hours or until the task is checked, left or reopened; see `dist/pd3-run.mjs`.
- Word counter of the writing meter (`countWords`): `hej,med,dig` (commas without spaces) counts as one word; this is left as is. The dictation comparison splits such text at the commas.
- The monologue timer starts when the recording has started; without a microphone it starts anyway and says "таймер идёт без записи".

## Dictation cards in the spaced repetition (state.pd3.spell)

Sentences written wrongly in a dictation (`misspelled` / `missing` words of `dist/pd3-diff.mjs`) become cards of the ordinary FSRS repetition.

- Storage: optional `state.pd3.spell = [{id,dictId,idx,words,count,lastAt}]`, newest first, at most 200 records, at most 5 words (40 characters) each. `id` is always `spell-<dictId>-<idx>` and is also the key of the card in `state.reviews`. Texts and translations are not stored: they come from `pd3-dictation-data.mjs`. The field is absent until the first mistake, so progress that never used it (and its hash) is unchanged. Validator and size ceiling: `pd3CleanSpell`, `validatePd3`, `sealPd3` in `engine.mjs`; merge by id (larger `count`, newest `lastAt`, union of words): `mergeSpell` in `pd3-spell.mjs`, used by `mergePd3` (pd3-store.mjs) and `mergePd3Tabs` (pd3-merge.mjs).
- Source: `checkDictation` (pd3-page.mjs) calls `spellFindings` and `deps.spell.record` (app.mjs, `recordSpell` in pd3-spell.mjs) only for a non-empty check. Only sentences the learner tried (sentences mode: something typed; text mode: at least one word found) and that have wrong or missing words are recorded. Every mistake is an "Again" of the card (`recordAttempt` with `correct:false`: due in 10 minutes, FSRS memory lowered, a lapse). A later error-free dictation does not touch the record.
- Catalogue: `getCatalog` adds `{kind:'dictation', source:'pd3', tag:'Диктант-карточка', spellWords}` items for the records (cache signature includes them). They are in `getWordEntries` (own entry, never merged with a word of the same text) and so in the plan, "Знакомые слова" and the review page, but not in lessons, `trainingPool` or "Мои слова". `memory.mjs`: they do not count as the day's new words.
- Task: `kind:'dictate'`, `dictMode:'listen'` (sentence is read aloud, the learner writes it; correct = `compareDictation(...).exact`) or `'cloze'` (no Danish voice: one problem word hidden, that word is typed). The first listening is free, each further one (`SPELL_FREE_LISTENS`) is help and gives an "assisted" answer. `restoreSession` validates both forms; `session.plays` is optional.
- UI: PD3 hub ("На повторении из диктантов: N", "Повторить сейчас" starts `practice-spell`), progress page line, notice after a dictation check.
- The Worker bundle (`dist/server/index.js`) contains an older copy of `engine.mjs`: it must be rebuilt (`tools/build-worker.mjs`) at release so that it knows the `spell` field (until then the field travels as an unknown PD3 field).

## Mock exam ("Пробный экзамен")

Code: `pd3-mock.mjs` (pure logic: schedule, sets, deadlines, scoring, report, record, run validator), `pd3-mock-view.mjs` (markup), `pd3-mock-page.mjs` (controller inside the PD3 page: clock, stages, pause/exit, report, saving), history/plan/progress snippets in `pd3-system-view.mjs`.
Two modes. Full: Del 1 (25 min), Del 2 (65 min), reply to a letter (25 min) and task A/B (35 min) = 150 min written, a break, then the oral part of about 15 minutes (monologue 3, interview 4, interaction 4, discussion 4). Short: Del 1 (15 min), Del 2 with texts 1 and 3 only (20 min), a reply to a letter (30 min), a break, monologue and interview. The timer is "по формату" (every stage has an absolute deadline; at the end the stage closes by itself and keeps what was typed) or off. Each stage is closed with "Завершить этап" (confirmation, no way back); the break has no clock; pause stops the clock and hides the task; exit drops the exam (no record, no XP).
Sets: set n takes the n-th reading exam pair, letter, task, monologue, interview, interaction and discussion (shorter lists start again); the set the learner has used least is offered first (`setUsage`: exam attempts, finished writing/oral items, earlier mock exams of that set).
The stages reuse the practice screens: `examFindView`/`examTextView`/`oralView` with `embedded:true` (no header, list buttons or own finish button; `tasks` selects the Del 2 tasks) and the shared writing pieces `writingPromptHtml`, `writingBoxHtml`, `writingChecklistHtml`, `writingOptionPicker`; the controller reuses the recorder, the monologue timer and the interview/interaction actions of the page. The exam keeps its own writing draft and ticks (they never touch `state.pd3.writing`).
Report: reading is checked by the program (Del 1, Del 2, with a review of mistakes: your answer, the right one, why); writing is the self-assessment on the 4 `writingCriteria` axes and speaking on the 6 `oralCriteria` axes (a percent appears when all axes are marked), with the mechanical checks of the texts (words, linking words, repeats, requirements and checklist ticks) shown separately; there is no pass/fail forecast and no official score. Weak spots by reading task type, by writing requirements/checks and by the lowest oral axes; at most four recommendations (reading by level, writing, oral phrases, dictation about the frequent mistake when statistics exist, repeat the set).
Storage: the unfinished exam is in the run store (`danskdag.pd3.run.v1`, schema version 2; the other views are still written as version 1 and both are read; 24 h for the short mode and 48 h for the full one, counted from the last save; the report stays there until it is closed). Leaving the PD3 section keeps the exam, its clock keeps running; a stage whose deadline passed while the page was closed is closed with what was entered and the next stage starts from now.
Results: `state.pd3.mocks` is optional (absent until the first finished exam), at most 20 records by id `{at,updatedAt,mode,set,find,text,writing,oral,weak,minutes,stages,of}` (percents or null; writing/oral are self-assessments); the newest `updatedAt` wins when copies are merged (`mergeMocks`, `mergePd3Tabs`, `adoptRemoteState`), so export/import, recovery copies and the cloud carry them. XP: `PD3_XP.mock` = 30 (15 in the short mode), once per exam id through `awardPd3`, only when at least half of the stages were really worked on (answers present, a text of at least half the minimum, an oral stage closed by the learner after 30 s), the day limit still applies.

## Grammar practice ("Грамматика B2 · формат экзамена")

Code: `pd3-grammar.mjs` (pure logic: checking, scoring, category statistics, recommendations, merging, run validator), `pd3-grammar-view.mjs` (markup), `pd3-grammar-page.mjs` (controller inside the PD3 page: list, intro, task by task, timer, result, saving, XP). Data contract: `docs/pd3-grammar-schema.md`; content `pd3-grammar-a-data.mjs` / `pd3-grammar-b-data.mjs` (12 sets of 10 tasks, 12 categories), joined in `pd3-data.mjs` as `grammarSets` and `grammarCategories`. The hub card has its own action (`pd3-gram-open`, not `pd3-open`) and is hidden while the content has no sets.
Flow: a list of sets with a theme filter (every theme has its rule on a button) and "weak theme: practise" (the set with the most tasks of the theme); an intro with the timer choice (15 minutes, absolute deadline, or none); one task at a time (gap, cloze, fix, transform, order); "Проверить" shows the review (your answer, the right one, why), "Дальше" moves on, "Пропустить" counts as not answered; at the end the percent, the result by theme, up to three recommendations (themes with the lowest share; links only to lessons / reference tabs that exist, `GRAMMAR_LINKS`) and the list of mistakes.
Checking: `gap`/`cloze` by option index (a cloze is right only when every gap is); `fix` needs the marked word to be the wrong one AND a correction equal to one of `fixes`; `transform` and `order` are compared with `answers` through the shared `normalize` of `engine.mjs` (case, punctuation, extra spaces and invisible characters are ignored, word order matters), so an order variant with a fronted adverbial, a capital first word or commas on other tiles is accepted. After a wrong `transform` the learner may mark "Мой вариант тоже верный": no point, no XP, no category statistics, the task is marked "самопроверка" in the review. The markup never contains the right answer before the check.
Storage: optional `state.pd3.grammar = {sets:{<id>:{best,last,attempts,at}},cats:{<catId>:{ok,all}},updatedAt}` (validator `pd3CleanGrammar` and the size ceiling in `engine.mjs`; absent until a set is finished; a set with no answered task is not saved). Import takes the larger best/attempts per set and the category entry with more answers (the same copy twice counts once, `mergeGrammar`); tab merging adds each tab's new answers (`mergeGrammarTabs`). XP: `PD3_XP.grammar` = 15 through `awardPd3` (once per set id, only for a better result, from 30 percent, only with at least 5 answered tasks, shared day limit). Hub: "Частые ошибки по грамматике" (three themes with the largest share of mistakes, at least 10 answers) with "Потренировать"; the plan and the progress page list grammar as a fifth skill / activity with the weakest theme.
Unfinished set: `danskdag.pd3.run.v1` view `'grammar'` (version 1 record: index, answers incl. chosen tiles, done / skipped / self-checked ids, timer flag, absolute deadline), validated against the set (`validateGrammarFields`), 24 hours. After a reload the same task and the time left come back; a deadline that passed while the page was closed ends the set with what was entered.
The Worker bundle (`dist/server/index.js`) has an older copy of `engine.mjs`: rebuild it at release so that it knows `grammar` (until then the field travels as an unknown PD3 field).
